About us

A Singapore studio for numbers that need to work

Oz Excel Studio helps small and mid-sized companies get more out of the data they already have. Most businesses run on spreadsheets, and many of those spreadsheets are slow, fragile, or understood by only one person.

We fix that in two ways. As consultants, we review how you plan, budget and report, then build clean financial models and dashboards around your real processes. As trainers, we offer online coaching and self-paced courses so you and your team can build and maintain that work yourselves.

Every engagement starts with your actual files and your actual problems. No generic slides, no jargon.
